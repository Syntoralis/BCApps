## Getting started in 5 steps

1. **Install** the extension from the [Microsoft Marketplace]({{ site.marketplace_url }}).
2. **Activate** the trial license in Business Central (Extensions → Syntoralis FIFO Logistic → Activate).
3. **Configure** the locations involved ([setup guide](./setup-en.html)).
4. **Test** on a pilot pick (warehouse or inventory).
5. **Validate** results (rotation, lots, serial numbers, FEFO).

## Checklist

- [ ] Extension installed and activated.
- [ ] At least one location configured for FIFO.
- [ ] Pilot pick created and validated.
- [ ] Lots / serial numbers assigned correctly.
- [ ] FEFO verified on items with expiration dates.

## Common issues

- **No FIFO layer**: verify receipts were recorded (the engine does not handle receipts).
- **Non-FIFO pick**: the location is not configured for FIFO — see [Setup](./setup-en.html).
- **Lots not assigned**: verify lot tracking on the item card.
