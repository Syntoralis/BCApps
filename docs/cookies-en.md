# Cookie Policy

This documentation site (`syntoralis.github.io/BCApps`) uses cookies and similar technologies to measure audience and improve the user experience. No non-essential cookie is set without your consent.

## Non-essential cookies (subject to consent)

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics (GA4) | Audience measurement, 
anonymized statistics | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics (GA4) | User distinction | 24 h |
| `_clck`, `_clsk` | Microsoft Clarity | Heatmaps, session recordings, UX analysis | 1 year |

These cookies are only set after you accept via the consent banner.

## Essential cookies

No essential cookies are used by this site beyond the technical mechanisms required to display it (no persistent cookies).

## Consent management

You can change your choice at any time using the **"Cookie settings"** button at the bottom of the page. Your preference is stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`) and is not sent to a server.

- **Accept**: enables Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.
- **Refuse**: no non-essential cookie is set; analytics scripts are not loaded.

## Legal basis

Non-essential cookies are set on the basis of your consent (Art. 6.1.a and 7 GDPR; Art. 82 French Data Protection Act / ePrivacy directive).
